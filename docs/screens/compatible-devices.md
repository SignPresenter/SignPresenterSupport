## Works with any Android 10+ device

SignPresenter works on any Android device running **Android 10.0 or newer**, including phones, tablets, and streaming boxes. [Download from Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.signpresenter.screen&pcampaignid=web_share).

This includes many **smart TVs** with Google Play or Fire OS built in — just search for Sign Presenter in your TV's app store.

:::tip Outside the USA?
The [Google TV Streamer](https://store.google.com/us/product/google_tv_streamer) is available internationally and works the same way — install SignPresenter directly from Google Play, no workarounds needed.
:::

## What about Fire Sticks?

### Older Fire Sticks (Fire OS) — fully supported

If you already own a Fire Stick running **Fire OS** (Android-based), it continues to work with SignPresenter. No changes needed.

### Newer Fire Sticks (Vega OS) — limited support

The newest Fire Sticks run **Vega OS** instead of Fire OS. SignPresenter runs on Vega OS but with these limitations:

- **Vertical screen mode does not work** — horizontal content works fine
- **Does not auto-reboot** after a restart or when the Home button is pressed

For a reliable, fully-supported setup we recommend the onn. devices above rather than purchasing a new Fire Stick.
